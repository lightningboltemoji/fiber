Lisbon’s best coffee, mapped

From the marble counters of the old leitarias to the kiosks on the miradouros: eight cafés worth the climb, and what to order at each.

RMBy Rui Matos · Updated 3 October 2026

8 cafés, in our order

  1. 1

    Torra Príncipe Real

    A roaster’s bar by the garden, and the city’s best flat white.

  2. 2

    Pastelaria Bica Doce Chiado

    Stand at the counter for a bica and a nata, still warm.

  3. 3

    Café Andorinha Graça

    Tiled walls, swallows on the cups, the 28 rattling past.

  4. 4

    Café do Cais Cais do Sodré

    A galão on the river wall while the ferries come in.

  5. 5

    Leitaria do Largo Alfama

    A 1920s dairy bar: a marble counter, a galão and a queijada.

  6. 6

    Quiosque do Monte Senhora do Monte

    The kiosk at the top of the city. Worth every step.

  7. 7

    Café Rosa Mouraria

    Three tables on a stepped lane, and a cake that changes daily.

  8. 8

    Café Arcada Baixa

    Under the arcades since 1911, with waiters in white jackets.
