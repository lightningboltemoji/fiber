Lisbon, in October

Dates
Fri, Oct 16 → Tue, Oct 20 · 4 nights
Stay
Casa do Alecrim, Alfama

Four nights, no rental car, mostly on foot. One plan per day and the rest of it left to the city. Everything is walkable from the flat except Sintra and Belém, and those are a train and a tram away.

Day by day

Fri 16arrive
Land at 7:05. Drop bags at Casa do Alecrim, sleep for two hours, then a slow loop: the Sé, Santa Luzia, and dinner at Tasca do Alecrim before 8.
Sat 17Alfama
Walk from the Sé up to Graça. Lunch with Inês at 1. Sunset at Senhora do Monte, then fado in Mouraria at 8.

Before we go

Book Casa do Alecrim

Eat

  • Tasca do Alecrim, Alfama. Sardines, vinho verde from the barrel, no reservations.
  • Cervejaria do Cais. Clams, garlic prawns, a prego at the counter to finish.
  • Pastelaria Estrela, Belém. Before 11, or don't bother.
  • Ginjinha in Rossio, standing up, in a chocolate cup if they have them.
