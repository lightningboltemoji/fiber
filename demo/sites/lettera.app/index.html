You asked for the real list, so here it is. Rule of thumb: ignore anything with a laminated menu out front.

  2. Cervejaria do Cais for clams and garlic prawns. Finish with a prego at the counter, like everyone else does.
  3. Senhora do Monte at sunset, not Santa Justa. Bring a bottle and get there 30 minutes early.
